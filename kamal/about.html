About Matruveda

The belief behind Matruveda.

Matruveda was founded on one belief: to raise a healthy, intelligent, sensitive and emotionally balanced child, the parents’ preparation must begin before conception. It looks at the whole development of the next generation, with a scientific eye.

“एक मूल जन्माला घालणे म्हणजे देवाची मूर्ती घडविणे होय.”

साने गुरुजी · To bring a child into the world is to shape an idol of God.

Our philosophy

Parenthood begins long before conception.

A child is first created in the minds and intentions of the parents, long before the body begins its work. When both parents prepare their body, mind, emotions and daily life, a healthy, balanced and well-rooted child follows. That is the whole idea of Garbhasanskar, and it is why every Matruveda program starts with the mother’s own wellbeing.

शुद्ध बीजापोटी फळे रसाळ गोमटी

When the seed is pure and the soil is nurtured with care, life naturally unfolds into health, balance and abundance.

Inspired by the philosophy of Manashakti Research Centre, Lonavala, and guided by Yog Vidya Gurukul.

The women behind Matruveda

Two women, yoga and science, one purpose.

ऋतुजा देशपांडे

Rutuja Deshpande

Co-Founder · Yoga Therapist, Professor of Yogashastra

Professor in the M.A. Yogashastra department at D.E.S.'s Willingdon College. B.Sc. Chemistry, M.A. Yogashastra, certified by the Yoga Certification Board (levels 1, 2 and 3), with specialised training in prenatal and postnatal care and Garbhasanskar from Manashakti Research Centre. She has guided more than 200 women through pregnancy and postpartum wellness programs.

योग शिक्षण, कुटुंब मार्गदर्शन आणि मूल्याधारित व्यक्तिमत्त्व विकास, २५ वर्षांहून अधिक काळ.

हर्षदा सहस्रबुद्धे

Harshada Sahasrabudhe

Co-Founder · Biotechnologist, Yoga Therapist, Garbhasanskar educator

M.Sc. Biotechnology, more than 10 years in clinical research and 15 years in yoga and holistic wellness. Certified Yoga Therapist, Meditation Coach, Reiki Healer and Garbhasanskar educator, trained at Manashakti Research Centre and Yog Vidya Gurukul. She supports women through menstrual health, stress, preconception, pregnancy, postpartum recovery and Balsanskar.
