Why this works

Learning does not begin at birth.

By the second month the baby's nervous system is already forming. Long before the first day, the mother's rhythms are the child's whole world: her breath, her sleep, her movement, her calm. That world is the baby's first teacher.

Garbhasanskar is that world made deliberate. One hour a day in which the mother is cared for on purpose, and the baby is spoken to, sung to and included. Yoga keeps the body able. The science decides what belongs in the hour, and when.

  • The mother's state reaches the babyCortisol crosses the placenta. A calm hour is not only calm for her.
  • The baby is already listeningHearing works well before birth, and a familiar voice is the first thing recognised after it.
  • What is safe changes every monthA practice that suits week 8 is not the one that suits week 30, which is why the class is live.

श्वास: ४, ४, ६ Breathing, 4-4-6

श्वास घ्या ४ सेकंद, थांबा ४, सोडा ६. कमळ उमलते आणि मिटते.

In for 4, hold 4, out for 6. The lotus opens and closes with you.

Ready?
ॐ ओंकाराचा नाद Om chanting

दीर्घ श्वास घ्या आणि वर्तुळ पूर्ण होईपर्यंत हळू ओंकार म्हणा. सहा वेळा.

Breathe in, then chant a slow Om until the ring completes. Six rounds.

Ready?
गर्भसंवाद Talking to your baby

दोन मिनिटे. हात पोटावर, श्वास शांत, आणि बाळाशी चार वाक्ये.

Two minutes. Hands on the belly, breath settled, four sentences to your baby.

१

हात हळूवार पोटावर ठेवा. डोळे मिटा.

Place your hands gently on the belly. Close your eyes.
२

तीन दीर्घ श्वास. पोट श्वासाबरोबर वर-खाली होऊ द्या.

Three slow breaths. Let the belly rise and fall.
३

मनात म्हणा: बाळा, मी इथे आहे.

Say quietly: little one, I am here.
४

म्हणा: तू सुरक्षित आहेस. तुझी वाट पाहते आहे.

Say: you are safe. I am waiting for you.
५

म्हणा: आजचा दिवस तुझ्यासाठी शांत असू दे.

Say: may today be peaceful for you.
६

एक शेवटचा श्वास. हळू डोळे उघडा.

One last breath. Open your eyes slowly.
